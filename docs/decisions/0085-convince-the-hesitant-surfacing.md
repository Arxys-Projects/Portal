# 0085 — Convince-the-hesitant surfacing

Status: Accepted (2026-07-20 — portal + PDF pieces implemented; badge/support copy pending Andy's wording review; "build the case" packet remains deferred to Phase 2)
Deciders: Andy Newbom
Relates to: the VMS Server Comparison tool and its downloadable PDF, the price book, the quote documents. Depends on 0084 for placement.

## Context

The portal serves the already-committed partner well and does little to move a partner who hasn't chosen Arxys. The convince content mostly already exists but is buried: the VMS Server Comparison carries the lead-time story (3-4 weeks vs 4-16), the Toshiba-allocation-into-2027 line, deployment margin math, honest deltas, and the "second source, no commitment, run one project and compare" framing, all under a Reference card. The downloadable comparison PDF is weaker than the on-screen tool because it drops those market-reality banners, so the artifact a partner carries to their customer loses the best of the argument. The PO-acceptance price lock, a real difference from Dell and HPE 2026 terms, appears nowhere in the UI.

## Decision

- **Elevate the VMS Server Comparison** to the first-class "win a job" destination (placement via 0084) with an entry aimed at the not-yet-committed partner.
- **Strengthen the downloadable comparison PDF** so the leave-behind carries the same lead-time, supply-stability, and second-source content the screen shows.
- **Surface the PO price lock** as a badge on quotes and the price book, worded on **Arxys's acceptance of the PO**, not the buyer issuing it.
- **Add honest support-model and durability content** (NBD advanced parts with self-repair, 8-5 Pacific, no phone/24-7; 5-year advanced-replacement warranty and multi-year framing).
- **Move the VMS validation sheets** onto the VMS Server Comparison page. *(Resolved 2026-07-16: the client confirmed the vendor chips download VMS-validated PDFs and belong with the switch decision; implemented as a three-button download row in the step card, removed from Quick Compare.)*
- **Extend co-branding (partner logo) to the System Estimate PDF**, not just the Phase 2 packet — same logo asset and render pattern serves both (addendum from the 2026-07-20 gap-analysis session; the estimate is the document partners generate most and present as their own).
- **Defer** the larger "build the case for your customer" packet to Phase 2 (its own strategy session), alongside new-partner onboarding and empty-state work.

## Consequences

- Copy for the badge, support, and durability content is written to the plain-voice / no-ai-slop / validated-not-certified rails, by Andy and Claude, not generated by the Design tool. Design owns placement and component; Code implements.
- The comparison-PDF change is a Code task extending the existing document template.
- Market-reality claims are perishable (lead-time gap, allocation date, competitor specs). Freshness is owned by Andy on a roughly quarterly cadence; no separate process introduced.

## Gates

- Validation-sheet move blocked until the compare-page vendor-chip behavior is confirmed.
- Price-lock wording reviewed against the quote T&C so it reads on acceptance, not issuance.
